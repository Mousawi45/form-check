# Form Check Live

Live rep counting and form checks from your phone camera, for the Daily Vitals gym tracker.

- Pose tracking runs on the phone (YOLO11n-pose via TensorFlow.js, WebGL). Video never leaves the device.
- Each exercise is judged against an evidence-based standard. A rep is **full**, a **stretch partial** (stretched end reached, squeezed end missed; counts, since growth research rates it about as good as a full rep) or a **partial** (stretched end missed). Form flags and plan cues (tempo, pauses, squeezes) are shown with their evidence level and sources.
- **Go live with today's workout** in Daily Vitals opens this page with that day's exercises, sets and rep ranges. Results carry the exercise slot, so pasting a code in Daily Vitals → Gym lands it on the right exercise.
- Search any of the 69 exercises from the exercise button.

Open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen.
